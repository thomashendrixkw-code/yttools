# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## What is expected

- Treat everyone with respect, regardless of who they are or how much they know.
- Give feedback on the work: what is wrong, why, and what would be better.
- Accept feedback the same way. Being wrong about something is not a character flaw.
- Ask questions. Nobody is expected to already know how this project works.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, or commits.
- Demeaning comments about someone's identity, background, or experience level.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, reopening settled decisions to
  relitigate them, or demanding unpaid work from maintainers.

## Scope

This applies anywhere the project is being discussed — issues, pull requests,
commit messages, and discussions.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through GitHub's private reporting on this repository. Reports
are handled confidentially. Depending on severity, the response ranges from a
private word to a permanent ban from the project.

Maintainers who do not follow this are subject to it too.
